About Kimberley

Kimberley is the capital city of South Africa’s Northern Cape. The city grew during the diamond rush of the late 19th century, and its mining history remains part of the place’s identity.

Today, visitors can explore Kimberley’s heritage, local attractions, food, accommodation, and activities. Explore Kimberley brings local experiences together to help visitors discover what the city and surrounding area have to offer.
