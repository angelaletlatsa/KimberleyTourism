How It Works

Explore Kimberley helps visitors discover local tourism experiences and connect with the people who provide them.

1. Browse experiences

Explore published attractions, activities, places to stay, and food experiences. Open a listing to see its description, location, price information, and contact or booking details.

2. Create an account to plan

Register for an account to access the visitor tools available on the platform, such as saving experiences and organizing your trip.

3. Choose what works for your visit

Review each listing’s details and availability before making plans. Booking and contact options depend on the experience.
